3. FII / DII / Pro / Client OI
Participant OI system ka base structural input hai.

Current day ka Open Interest previous valid trading day ke Open Interest se compare hota hai.

OI change ka meaning
OI pattern	Meaning	Directional effect
Long OI up + Short OI down	Fresh longs / short pressure reduce	Bullish pressure
Short OI up + Long OI down	Fresh shorts / long exit	Bearish pressure
Short OI down	Short covering	Bullish, but fresh long se weak
Long OI down	Long unwinding	Bearish, but fresh short se weak
Participant role
Pro
Near-term next-day structure mein useful input.
Pro ka fresh index futures positioning short-term context de sakta hai.
Lekin Pro alone guaranteed direction nahi deta.
FII
Carry aur broader derivative positioning context.
FII activity important hai, but single-day FII number ko guaranteed signal nahi maana ja sakta.
Client
Crowd positioning ka input.
Kabhi-kabhi contrarian risk batata hai.
Primary trend signal nahi.
DII F&O
Direct directional score mein limited ya zero weight.
Reason: standalone stable edge prove nahi hua.
FII vs Pro conflict
Agar FII aur Pro opposite direction suggest karte hain:

confidence reduce hoti hai,
reversal confirmation chahiye,
WAIT / NO TRADE more appropriate ho sakta hai.
4. Participant volume
OI batata hai ki positions active kitni hain.
Participant volume batata hai ki activity kitni hui.

Possible interpretation
High volume + fresh long OI = conviction ka possible sign
High volume + fresh short OI = bearish conviction ka possible sign
High volume + little net OI change = churn / two-sided trading / range risk
Low volume + strong OI reading = false move risk
Participant volume useful context hai, but standalone 80% directional edge prove nahi hua.

5. FII / DII cash flow
Cash flow derivative OI ko confirm ya contradict kar sakta hai.

Example:

Derivative OI bullish ho
But cash FII selling strong ho
To bullish setup weak ho sakta hai.

Cash flow ka use:

confirmation
divergence detection
confidence reduce/increase
risk context
Cash flow ko automatic BUY/SELL signal nahi maana jana chahiye.

Positive OI change = build-up
Negative OI change = unwind

Put unwind = support weak ho sakta hai

9. Sweep, wick aur trap logic
Market kabhi level ke upar/neeche jaa kar stops trigger karta hai aur phir reverse karta hai.

Isko sweep/trap context mein dekha jata hai.

Bull trap
Price resistance ke upar nikle
Sustain na kare
Wapas resistance ke neeche aa jaye
Failed reclaim aaye
Possible bearish trap context.

Bear trap
Price support ke neeche jaye
Sustain na kare
Wapas support ke upar reclaim kare
Possible bullish trap context.

Wick alone enough nahi.
Close aur follow-through confirmation necessary hai.

10. V10 tiny-gap previous-close-touch module

Condition:

Weekly forecast nahi
Automatic options trade nahi
Guaranteed profit nahi
V10 mein prior-close touch ka historical result strong tha, but execution target/stop/premium analysis mein robust standalone trade prove nahi hua.

17. Accuracy backtest rules
Agar target 80%+ hai, to test honest hona chahiye.

Wrong method
Same historical period par repeatedly rules tune karna
Best result choose karke claim karna
Future data feature mein use karna
Sign-only accuracy ko full exact prediction bolna
5 trades/sample ko 80% edge bolna
Correct method
Earlier data = development
Middle unseen data = validation
Later unseen data = confirmation
New future live data = forward test
Exact UP/FLAT/DOWN accuracy separate
Non-FLAT sign accuracy separate
Coverage report mandatory
Skipped days and skip reason mandatory
Execution test separate
Fresh forward result pass hone ke baad hi production promotion
Current session-count plan
80 complete sessions: data quality review only
200 complete sessions: first frozen study
260 complete sessions: fresh-forward promotion check
Intraday ke 20–26 rows ek day mein ho sakte hain, but woh 20 independent samples nahi hain. Ek trading day ko ek independent session treat karna chahiye.
