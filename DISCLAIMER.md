# Financial and Data Disclaimer

This software is for education, research, and decision support. It is not personalized investment advice, a SEBI-registered recommendation, or a guarantee of accuracy or profit.

Market data may be delayed, revised, unavailable, blocked, incomplete, or inconsistent across providers. Fallback and quality controls reduce risk but cannot guarantee uninterrupted or correct data.

Futures and options are leveraged instruments and can cause rapid, substantial losses. A correct directional forecast may still lose money because of premium decay, implied-volatility changes, spreads, slippage, fees, execution delays, gap risk, and position sizing.

Do not deploy capital solely because a raw model probability is high. Use the production decision only after data, date, research-promotion, event, liquidity, and execution gates pass. Missing or failed evidence must remain WAIT / NO TRADE.

Long-term investment decisions require separate fundamental analysis, valuation, diversification, time horizon, and personal risk assessment.
